Overview

Eight species at the end of the classification pathway

The revised species set was selected to keep the required two-family, four-genus and eight-species structure while making the visual side of the website much stronger. Dipterocarpaceae contributes four large rainforest trees, while Orchidaceae contributes four flowering orchids with distinctive forms and colours.

The collection remains closely tied to Malaysia. Several plants are native to Malaysian Borneo, including Paphiopedilum rothschildianum in Sabah, Paphiopedilum sanderianum in Sarawak and Phalaenopsis bellina in Borneo. Other species occur in Peninsular Malaysia or across the wider Malesian region. The photographs make it easier to compare growth form, leaves, flowers and habitat rather than relying on generic illustrations.

Dipterocarpaceae → Dipterocarpus

Dipterocarpus species

Species

Dipterocarpus grandiflorus

A large wet-tropical forest tree with distinctive winged fruits. Its native range includes Malaya and Borneo, making it a strong example of the dipterocarp forests that characterise much of Malaysia.

Family: Dipterocarpaceae
Genus: Dipterocarpus

Taxonomy source: Plants of the World Online — Dipterocarpus grandiflorus.

Species

Dipterocarpus baudii

A tall dipterocarp tree native to tropical Asia, including Malaya. The species is useful for the Explorer because clear photographs show its broad leaves, trunk and forest habit.

Family: Dipterocarpaceae
Genus: Dipterocarpus

Taxonomy source: Plants of the World Online — Dipterocarpus baudii.

Dipterocarpaceae → Shorea

Shorea species

Species

Shorea leprosula

A red-meranti dipterocarp of lowland tropical forest. It is widespread enough to be well photographed while still representing the Malaysian dipterocarp flora clearly.

Family: Dipterocarpaceae
Genus: Shorea

Taxonomy source: Plants of the World Online — Shorea leprosula.

Species

Shorea macrophylla

A Bornean dipterocarp commonly associated with engkabang or illipe nuts. Its large leaves and fruit make it visually distinct from the other tree species in this collection.

Family: Dipterocarpaceae
Genus: Shorea

Taxonomy source: Plants of the World Online — Shorea macrophylla.

Orchidaceae → Paphiopedilum

Paphiopedilum species

Species

Paphiopedilum rothschildianum

A spectacular slipper orchid native to Sabah. Its striped pouch-shaped flowers and long horizontal petals make it one of the most visually recognisable plants in the Explorer.

Family: Orchidaceae
Genus: Paphiopedilum

Taxonomy source: Plants of the World Online — Paphiopedilum rothschildianum.

Species

Paphiopedilum sanderianum

A rare Sarawak slipper orchid famous for extraordinarily long twisting lateral petals. Its restricted Bornean range gives the Orchidaceae branch a strong local Malaysian connection.

Family: Orchidaceae
Genus: Paphiopedilum

Taxonomy source: Plants of the World Online — Paphiopedilum sanderianum.

Orchidaceae → Phalaenopsis

Phalaenopsis species

Species

Phalaenopsis bellina

An epiphytic Bornean orchid with waxy greenish flowers marked by vivid magenta-purple colour near the centre. It provides a compact flowering contrast to the large forest trees in the Explorer.

Family: Orchidaceae
Genus: Phalaenopsis

Taxonomy source: Plants of the World Online — Phalaenopsis bellina.

Species

Phalaenopsis violacea

A fragrant epiphytic orchid native from Peninsular Malaysia to Sumatra. Its purple-toned flowers are easy to distinguish visually and complement the Bornean Phalaenopsis species.

Family: Orchidaceae
Genus: Phalaenopsis

Taxonomy source: Plants of the World Online — Phalaenopsis violacea.
