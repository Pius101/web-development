1 Aisha Rahman Dipterocarpus grandiflorus Kubah National Park, Sarawak Observed a mature dipterocarp beside a shaded forest trail. 2 Daniel Lee Phalaenopsis bellina Matang, Sarawak Found this epiphytic orchid on a tree in a humid shaded area. The waxy green flower had a vivid magenta centre that was easy to recognise. Aisha Rahman: The flower colour makes this one especially easy to compare with other orchids.

Nur Izzati: Good note about it growing as an epiphyte rather than in the soil.		 Open
3 Mei Tan Shorea leprosula Taman Negara, Pahang A tall red-meranti tree photographed from the walking path. The straight trunk continued well above the lower forest vegetation. Farid Hassan: The trunk height gives a good sense of how dominant these trees can be.

Daniel Lee: This would be useful to compare with younger Shorea trees.		 Open
4 Farid Hassan Dipterocarpus baudii Ulu Gombak, Selangor Observed in lowland forest beside a trail. The broad ribbed leaves were the feature that first made me stop and photograph the tree. Aisha Rahman: The leaf detail is useful for comparing it with other Dipterocarpus species.

Siti Aminah: I like that you recorded which feature caught your attention first.		 Open
5 Nur Izzati Paphiopedilum rothschildianum Kinabalu area, Sabah Photographed a slipper orchid in a cool humid mountain setting. The striped pouch and long horizontal petals were its most obvious features. Mei Tan: The flower shape is very distinctive and makes the record easy to recognise.

Jason Wong: The mountain habitat note adds useful context to the photograph.		 Open
6 Jason Wong Shorea macrophylla Batang Ai, Sarawak A Bornean dipterocarp observed near the forest edge. I photographed the large leaves and fruit so the record could be compared with other engkabang trees. Farid Hassan: Showing both leaves and fruit makes the record much more useful.

Nur Izzati: The Sarawak location is a good fit for this Bornean species.		 Open

8 Amir Hakim Paphiopedilum sanderianum Gunung Mulu, Sarawak Observed a slipper orchid with exceptionally long twisting petals. The unusual flower form was visible even from a short distance. Siti Aminah: Those long petals are an excellent identifying feature.

Mei Tan: The Mulu location is useful context for such a locally restricted orchid.		 Open
9 Aisha Rahman Dipterocarpus grandiflorus Bako, Sarawak A second Dipterocarpus grandiflorus record from a different site. This tree was close to a trail with several winged fruits visible below the canopy. Farid Hassan: It is useful to have another record of the same species from a different site.
