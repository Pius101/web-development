Contribution Guidelines

Good observations and respectful comments are easier for other visitors to understand and compare.

  • Use a clear photograph of the plant.
  • Provide the plant name if it is known.
  • Keep observation comments relevant.
  • Respond politely to other contributors.
  • Avoid unnecessary personal information.

How it works

From observation to community discussion

Contributors share plant observations, add useful notes and take part in respectful discussions about what they have seen. Contributor profiles bring each person's observations together in one place.

  1. Submit an observation using the Contribution page.
  2. Add clear plant details including a name, photograph and location.
  3. Share a useful note describing what you noticed about the plant or habitat.
  4. Visit contributor profiles to browse observations grouped by community member.
  5. Read community replies to see what other contributors noticed or compared.

Contribution terms

Understanding each community record

Contributor
The person who submits the plant observation to the project.
Plant observation
A record describing a plant that a contributor has encountered and chosen to share.
Contributor comment
The original observation note supplied with the plant record.
Community comment
A reply from another contributor discussing or comparing the submitted observation.
