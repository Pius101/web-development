Overview

From families to closer relatives

A genus is more specific than a family. Under Dipterocarpaceae, the Explorer uses Dipterocarpus and Vatica. Under Myristicaceae, it uses Horsfieldia and Knema. All four genera are listed in the supplied PlantsData.

The four genera are tropical woody plants with strong links to Malaysia and the wider Malesian region. Moving from the family page to this page narrows the classification before the Species page identifies the eight individual plants.

  1. Use the family label to identify the broader group.
  2. Compare the two genera inside each family.
  3. Follow a species link to reach the most specific level.

Family: Dipterocarpaceae

Dipterocarpus and Vatica

Genus

Dipterocarpus

Native-range focus: Tropical Asia, including Malaya and Borneo.

Dipterocarpus contains large resinous forest trees distributed through tropical Asia. The selected species are D. grandiflorus and D. kunstleri.

Taxonomy source: Plants of the World Online — Dipterocarpus.

View Dipterocarpus species →
Genus

Vatica

Native-range focus: Malaysia and Borneo.

Vatica is a genus of tropical trees in Dipterocarpaceae. PlantsData explicitly lists Vatica and includes both V. borneensis and V. flavida among its example species.

Taxonomy source: Plants of the World Online — Vatica.

View Vatica species →

Family: Myristicaceae

Horsfieldia and Knema

Genus

Horsfieldia

Native-range focus: Malaysia and western Malesia.

Horsfieldia is a genus of evergreen tropical trees in Myristicaceae. The selected species are H. flocculosa and H. penangiana, both recorded from Malaysia.

Taxonomy source: Plants of the World Online — Horsfieldia.

View Horsfieldia species →
Genus

Knema

Native-range focus: Malaya and western Malesia.

Knema is a genus of tropical trees in Myristicaceae. Its fruits often split to expose a brightly coloured seed covering. The selected species are K. communis and K. mandaharan.

Taxonomy source: Plants of the World Online — Knema.

Key terms

Genus
A group of closely related species within a family.
Native range
The geographic area where a plant occurs naturally.
Aril
