Why families matter

A family is a broad taxonomic group. It helps visitors understand how the four selected genera are related before moving to individual species.

Overview

Two Malaysian tree families

MY Plant Watch uses Dipterocarpaceae and Myristicaceae, both of which appear in the supplied PlantsData. Dipterocarpaceae contains the selected genera Dipterocarpus and Vatica, while Myristicaceae contains Horsfieldia and Knema.

The family level is the broadest level used in this Explorer. From each family, visitors can move to two genera and then to two species under each genus. This produces the required hierarchy of two families, four genera and eight species.

  1. Choose one of the two families below.
  2. Open the linked genera for that family.
  3. Continue from a genus to its two selected species.

Explore the two family groups

Family

Dipterocarpaceae

Regional focus: Malaysia and tropical Southeast Asia.

Dipterocarpaceae is a major tropical tree family and an important component of Malaysian rainforests. This Explorer follows Dipterocarpus and Vatica, two genera listed under the family in PlantsData.

Taxonomy source: Plants of the World Online — Dipterocarpaceae.

View its genera →
Family

Myristicaceae

Regional focus: Malaysia and the wider Malesian region.

Myristicaceae is the nutmeg family, a tropical family of woody plants. PlantsData lists Horsfieldia, Knema and Myristica under this family; this Explorer uses the first two genera.

Taxonomy source: Plants of the World Online — Myristicaceae.
