Overview

Eight species at the end of the classification pathway

The species page completes the required two-family, four-genus and eight-species hierarchy. Dipterocarpaceae contributes species of Dipterocarpus and Vatica, while Myristicaceae contributes species of Horsfieldia and Knema.

All eight plants are connected to Malaysia or the wider Malesian region. The Vatica choices include two species named directly in PlantsData, while the remaining species were selected under genera that are also listed in the supplied document.

  1. Use each genus heading to identify the closest relatives.
  2. Compare the native-range summary for each species.
  3. Use the family and genus links to move back up the hierarchy.

Dipterocarpaceae → Dipterocarpus

Dipterocarpus species

Species

Dipterocarpus grandiflorus

Native range: Malaya, Borneo and other parts of tropical Southeast Asia.

A large wet-tropical forest tree with distinctive winged fruits. Its native range includes Malaya and Borneo.

Family: Dipterocarpaceae
Genus: Dipterocarpus

Taxonomy source: Plants of the World Online — Dipterocarpus grandiflorus.

Species

Dipterocarpus kunstleri

Native range: Borneo, Malaya, the Philippines and Sumatra.

A wet-tropical dipterocarp tree whose native range includes both Peninsular Malaysia and Borneo.

Family: Dipterocarpaceae
Genus: Dipterocarpus

Taxonomy source: Plants of the World Online — Dipterocarpus kunstleri.

Dipterocarpaceae → Vatica

Vatica species

Species

Vatica borneensis

Native range: Borneo, including Sarawak.

A wet-tropical tree native to Borneo, with records from Brunei and Sarawak. It is one of the species explicitly listed under Vatica in PlantsData.

Family: Dipterocarpaceae
Genus: Vatica

Taxonomy source: Plants of the World Online — Vatica borneensis.

Species

Vatica flavida

Native range: Peninsular Malaysia, especially southern Perak.

A wet-tropical tree native to Peninsular Malaysia. It is another species explicitly listed under Vatica in PlantsData.

Family: Dipterocarpaceae
Genus: Vatica

Taxonomy source: Plants of the World Online — Vatica flavida.

Myristicaceae → Horsfieldia

Horsfieldia species

Species

Horsfieldia flocculosa

Native range: Peninsular Malaysia to eastern Sumatra.

A wet-tropical tree recorded from Peninsular Malaysia to eastern Sumatra. It belongs to the Myristicaceae branch selected from PlantsData.

Family: Myristicaceae
Genus: Horsfieldia

Taxonomy source: Plants of the World Online — Horsfieldia flocculosa.

Species

Horsfieldia penangiana

Native range: Peninsular Malaysia to Sumatra.

A tropical tree native from Peninsula Malaysia to Sumatra, with accepted infraspecific records extending within western Malesia.

Family: Myristicaceae
Genus: Horsfieldia

Taxonomy source: Plants of the World Online — Horsfieldia penangiana.

Myristicaceae → Knema

Knema species

Species

Knema communis

Native range: Malaya.

A wet-tropical tree native to Malaya. Its genus is listed under Myristicaceae in PlantsData.

Family: Myristicaceae
Genus: Knema

Taxonomy source: Plants of the World Online — Knema communis.

Species

Knema mandaharan

Native range: Peninsular Malaysia to Sumatra.

A wet-tropical tree whose native range extends from Peninsular Malaysia to Sumatra.

Family: Myristicaceae
Genus: Knema

Taxonomy source: Plants of the World Online — Knema mandaharan.
