About MY Plant Watch

What is MY Plant Watch?

MY Plant Watch is a citizen-science website for discovering Malaysian plants, understanding how they are classified and exploring observations shared by the community. Visitors can move from broad plant families to genera and species, use the identification interface and contribute their own plant observations.

Featured plants

Featured species

Dipterocarpaceae

Dipterocarpus grandiflorus

A large rainforest tree with broad leaves and distinctive winged fruits, native across parts of tropical Southeast Asia including Malaysia.

Dipterocarpaceae

Vatica borneensis

A Bornean dipterocarp tree recorded from Brunei and Sarawak, growing mainly in wet tropical forest.

Myristicaceae

Horsfieldia flocculosa

A Myristicaceae tree native from Peninsular Malaysia to eastern Sumatra, with large leaves typical of the genus Horsfieldia.

Myristicaceae

Knema communis

A Myristicaceae tree native to Malaya. Knema species are notable for fruits that open to reveal brightly coloured seeds.

Malaysia's plant life

Why Malaysian plants matter

Malaysia's forests, mountains and lowlands support an extraordinary range of plant life. Native trees and other plants form habitats, support wildlife and are part of the natural character of the country's landscapes.

Learning to recognise plants and recording where they are observed can build biodiversity awareness. MY Plant Watch brings that idea into a simple citizen-science website by connecting classification, identification and community observations.
