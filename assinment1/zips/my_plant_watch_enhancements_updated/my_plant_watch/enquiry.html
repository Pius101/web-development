Plant enquiries

Ask About Malaysian Plants

Send an enquiry about a plant family, genus, species, identification or contribution. Complete every required field and your browser will check the information before submission.

Before you submit

Enquiry tips

  • Use letters and spaces only for first and last names.
  • Enter a valid email address.
  • Use a phone number containing up to 10 digits.
  • Select the plant classification area that best matches your question.
  • Give enough detail in the comments box for the enquiry to be understood.

All fields marked as required must be completed before the form can be submitted.
