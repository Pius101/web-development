Photo Tips

A useful identification photo should make the plant's visible features easy to inspect.

  • Use good natural lighting.
  • Keep the plant in focus.
  • Include leaves, flowers or fruit when possible.
  • Avoid heavy filters or effects.

How it works

From photograph to plant information

Use the identification page as a starting point when you encounter an unfamiliar plant. Clear photographs and accurate names make it easier to compare the plant with information in the Explorer.

  1. Select a photograph showing the plant clearly.
  2. Start identification using the Identify Plant button.
  3. Review the suggested result, including scientific name and classification.
  4. Read supporting information such as location, environment and care information.
  5. Continue to Explorer to compare the plant with related families, genera and species.

Identification terms

Useful terms on this page

Scientific name
The formal name used to identify a plant species consistently.
Common name
A familiar everyday name that may be used for a plant in a particular place or community.
Identification result
The plant information shown after an image or search request has been analysed.
Habitat
The natural environment or type of location in which a plant grows.
