Overview

From broad families to closer relatives

A genus is more specific than a family. The revised collection keeps two genera under each family so the structure remains balanced: Dipterocarpus and Shorea under Dipterocarpaceae, and Paphiopedilum and Phalaenopsis under Orchidaceae.

The four genera also create a useful visual contrast. The dipterocarp genera contain large rainforest trees, while the orchid genera contain epiphytic or terrestrial flowering plants with highly distinctive flowers. All four have species with natural distributions connected to Malaysia, including Sabah, Sarawak and Peninsular Malaysia.

Family: Dipterocarpaceae

Dipterocarpus and Shorea

Genus

Dipterocarpus

Dipterocarpus contains large resinous forest trees distributed through tropical Asia. The two selected species both occur in Malaysia and are well represented by field photographs.

Taxonomy source: Plants of the World Online — Dipterocarpus.

Genus

Shorea

Shorea is a familiar name for a large group of Southeast Asian dipterocarp trees, including many meranti and engkabang trees. The selected species represent both Peninsular and Bornean forest contexts.

Taxonomy source: Plants of the World Online — Shorea.

Family: Orchidaceae

Paphiopedilum and Phalaenopsis

Genus

Paphiopedilum

Paphiopedilum is the slipper-orchid genus, named for the pouch-like lip of its flowers. The two selected species are strongly associated with Malaysian Borneo and have striking, well-photographed flowers.

Taxonomy source: Plants of the World Online — Paphiopedilum.

View Paphiopedilum species →
Genus

Phalaenopsis

Phalaenopsis contains epiphytic orchids with broad leaves and showy flowers. The two selected species connect the collection to Borneo and Peninsular Malaysia.

Taxonomy source: Plants of the World Online — Phalaenopsis.

Key terms

Genus
A group of closely related species within a family.
Native range
The geographic area where a plant occurs naturally.
Epiphyte
A plant that grows on another plant for physical support without taking nutrients from it as a parasite.
